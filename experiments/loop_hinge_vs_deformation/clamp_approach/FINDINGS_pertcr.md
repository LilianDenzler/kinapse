# Per-TCR validation of the CDR-loop clamp

**Question.** The 22-TCR *averaged* analysis (`../simplified_graphs`) found each CDR loop is anchored on one
stem by a rigid flanking β-strand — the "clamp" (orange strands C/C′/F in
`loops_flanks_fluctuation_annotated.png`). Does that clamp hold **for every individual TCR**, or only on
average? Here we test each of the 22 unbound-TCR MD trajectories separately.

**Method (alignment-free, kinapse IMGT).** For every residue *i* of each CDR loop and its flanks we take
`fluct_to_rigid(i) = mean over the ultra-rigid core residues j (j≠i) of D_std(i,j)`, where `D_std` is the
std over MD frames of the CA_i–CA_j distance (cached per-TCR matrices in `../simplified_graphs/results_fluct`,
generated by kinapse `load_tcr`). A residue with `fluct_to_rigid ≤ 0.5 Å` does not move relative to the rigid
core → it is clamped. Ultra-rigid core from `rigid_framework.json` (α: 13 residues, β: 20). Clamp side per
CDR (from the averaged analysis): **CDR1 → C-stem, CDR2 → N-stem, CDR3 → N-stem**. Clamped/free stem =
the 3 terminal loop residues on each side.

*Integrity check:* a fresh kinapse `load_md("3QH3")` (IMGT renumbering, β-112.1 insertion preserved)
reproduces the cached `D_std` exactly (max |Δ| = 0.0 Å). Insertion codes (112A→112.1, 111A→111.1) are carried
through everywhere and never dropped.

## Result — the clamp holds TCR-by-TCR

Clamp-stem `fluct_to_rigid`, averaged over TCRs (per-TCR grids in the figures):

| region | clamp side | clamp mean (Å) | free mean (Å) | apex (Å) | pass ≤0.5 | clamp<free |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| α CDR1 | C | 0.36 | 0.58 | 0.75 | 19/22 | 22/22 |
| β CDR1 | C | 0.27 | 0.43 | — | 22/22 | 22/22 |
| α CDR2 | N | 0.40 | 0.53 | 0.53 | 20/22 | 20/22 |
| β CDR2 | N | 0.35 | 0.50 | 0.70 | 21/22 | 22/22 |
| α CDR3 | N | 0.26 | 0.38 | 0.63 | 22/22 | 22/22 |
| β CDR3 | N | 0.24 | 0.38 | 0.68 | 22/22 | 22/22 |

- **The clamped stem is the rigid, static side in essentially every TCR** (`clamp < free` in 20–22 of 22 per
  region; 128/132 region×TCR cells overall). The asymmetry is not an averaging artifact.
- **In absolute terms the clamped stem sits well inside the 0.5 Å rigidity criterion** (median 0.24–0.37 Å),
  i.e. it is genuinely locked to the ultra-rigid core in each trajectory. CDR3's N-clamp is the tightest
  (0.24–0.26 Å, the Cys104 + V-germline C-A-x anchor); CDR1/CDR2 clamps are slightly looser.
- **The 5 cells that cross 0.5 Å** (α CDR1: 1KGC 0.52, 3VXT 0.55, 6FRA 0.62; α CDR2: 3DX9 0.57, 3SKN 0.55;
  β CDR2: 3SKN 0.54) are driven by the **innermost** stem residue of a very short loop bleeding toward the
  apex — the per-residue profiles show the actual clamp *point* next to the strand (α38, α/β56, 105–107)
  stays pinned (≈0.2–0.3 Å) even in those TCRs. The clamp point never fails; only the 3-residue stem average
  drifts up where the loop is short.
- **The per-residue gradient is universal** (`clamp_pertcr_profiles.png`): every TCR shows the same shape —
  clamped base pinned to the core, mobility ramping up to the apex (β/α CDR3 apex is deformation-heavy, as
  before), and the free stem elevated. The clamp is a per-TCR structural invariant.
